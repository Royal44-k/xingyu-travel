# Asset provenance and usage

The five PNG files in this directory are original AI-generated project imagery created specifically for the Xingyu Travel public MVP with OpenAI's built-in Image Gen tool on 2026-08-16. They were not sourced from a third-party stock library, and no third-party reference images were supplied.

Every generation request prohibited text, logos, watermarks, fake interfaces, commercial marks, branded objects, and recognizable faces. The selected files were visually inspected for those constraints before being added to the project.

## Final prompt summaries

- `hero-dali-dawn.png`: Photorealistic editorial sunrise over Dali mountains and lake valley, with a winding shoreline as a leading line, bright detail on the center-right, and a dark uncluttered left third reserved for white title copy; restrained black, gold, and ivory palette.
- `destination-sichuan.png`: Low-camera editorial view across autumn grass on the western Sichuan plateau toward snow mountains, with layered depth and one tiny non-identifiable traveler; muted charcoal, ochre, gold, and ivory tones.
- `destination-guilin.png`: Guilin karst river at dusk with layered limestone peaks, warm reflections, dark natural foreground framing, and one distant bamboo raft operator shown only as an unidentifiable silhouette.
- `guide-dali-courtyard.png`: Intimate lived-in Bai courtyard in Dali with weathered white plaster, dark timber, plants, and a worn stone table in soft natural morning light; residential rather than commercial or resort styling.
- `guardian-rainy-mountain.png`: Safe, non-sensational view from behind a continuous guardrail along an intact wet highland road, with distant rain curtains, layered clouds, and a subtle warm break in the sky; no vehicles, people, damage, or disaster imagery.
